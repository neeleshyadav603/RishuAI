## Rishu v0.8.0 — offline brain on the phone too

**Downloads (below, under Assets):**
- `RishuSetup-0.8.0.exe` — Windows (offline brain inside, about 0.5 GB)
- `Rishu-0.8.0-android-arm64.apk` — Android, small (about 23 MB). The offline brain is downloaded once inside the app (about 430 MB, on Wi-Fi).
- `Rishu-0.8.0-android-arm64-offline.apk` — Android, offline brain inside (about 430 MB). Works without internet from the first start.

Install only one of the two APKs (either can replace the other). If an older Rishu is on the phone, uninstall it first (the APK is not signed with a permanent key yet).

### Install on Android
1. Download the APK on the phone, open it and allow installing from this source.
2. Open Rishu → **Laptop ke bina chalao** (or pair with the laptop).
3. Offline APK: the offline brain gets ready by itself on the first start (it is copied inside the phone once; keep about 1 GB free).
   Small APK: Settings → **Offline dimaag** → Download (once, on Wi-Fi).
4. Optional: add a free Groq key too. Then Rishu uses the fast online model and switches to the offline one by itself when there is no internet.

### New in 0.8.0
- **Offline brain on Android** (Qwen3 0.6B with llama.cpp, running on the phone itself): with no internet and no key Rishu still sets alarms and timers, opens apps, remembers things, chats and answers simple questions. Needs Android 9 or newer and a 64-bit phone; 4 GB RAM or more is best.
- **Automatic fallback:** with a Groq key Rishu uses the online model; if the internet goes or the free limit is used up, the phone's offline brain answers.

### Honest limits
- The small offline model is much weaker than the online models: for hard questions, research and long answers use the Groq key. Its first answer can take 10–30 seconds while it loads.
- Calls and WhatsApp messages still need your tap on Call/Send.
- Needs a test on your phone: speed and memory of the offline brain.

Everything from v0.7.0 (offline brain on Windows, phone without the laptop, documents, grading, Google Sheets, tidy folders, dictation, briefing, meeting notes, invoices, deadlines) is included.
